You gain a hit die. Your HP increases by **1d8+CON**.
## New Spells
You learn one extra cantrip and gain an extra level 2 spell slot (now up to 3)
## Wild Shape Improvement
You can now turn into things that can swim. You still **can't** turn into a flying creature.
## Cantrip Versatility
You can optionally switch out a known cantrip for another from the Druid cantrip list (only ones that contain Circle of the Moon or Druid in the sub class column).
![[Druid Spellbook#Cantrips]]
## Additional options
You can choose 1 of 3 options:
- Increase 1 ability score by 2 (max of 20)
- Increase 2 ability scores by 1 (max of 20)
- Take a feat
