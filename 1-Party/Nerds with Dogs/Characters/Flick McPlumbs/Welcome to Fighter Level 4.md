You gain a hit die. Your HP increases by **1d10+CON**.
## Martial Versatility (optional)
You can replace a fighting style you know with another fighting style available to fighters.
## Additional options
You can choose 1 of 3 options:
- Increase 1 ability score by 2 (max of 20)
- Increase 2 ability scores by 1 (max of 20)
- Take a feat
