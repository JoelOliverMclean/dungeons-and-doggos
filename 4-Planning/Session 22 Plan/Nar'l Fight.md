### Boss Battle: Nar'l (Level 3 Party)
- **Nar'l:** HP 38 | AC 15 (*Mage Armor*) | DC 13
  - *Key Spells:* Misty Step (1/1), Scorching Ray (1/1), Shield (Reactions)
- **Shadow Hounds (2):** HP 11 | AC 12 | Bite +4 (1d6+2)

### Nar'l Spells & Actions (Level 3 Capable)
- **Ignaroch's Retribution (Recharge 5-6):** 15ft Cone, DC 13 Dex. Deals 3d6 fire + 1d4 burn next turn.
- **Shatter (Hellfire):** 10ft Radius, DC 13 Con. Deals 3d8 Thunder/Fire.
- **Tasha's Mind Whip:** DC 13 Int. Deals 3d6 Psychic + restricts action economy.
- **Misty Step (1/1):** Bonus Action 30ft Teleport + 1d6 Fire to adjacent enemies upon leaving.

### Mid-Fight Triggers
- **Round 1:** Nar'l drops *Burning Hands* or *Thunderwave* on the front line, then uses *Misty Step* to retreat behind tree cover.
- **Mhazhique's Moment:** Trilbi offers the "Trial Bolt" (1d10 psychic + 1d4 attack penalty to target) if Mhazhique leans into the hat contract.
- **Ignaroch Reveal:** Nar'l's spells flare with dark hellfire, revealing Ignaroch's influence to anyone passing a DC 11 Arcana check.

### Trilbi's Combat Interventions (Initiative 20)
- **Whimsical Heal:** Spectral pie slaps a player -> Heals 2d4+2 HP + removes 1 condition.
- **Banana Peel Drop:** DC 12 Dex Save or target falls Prone + slides 10ft.
- **Confetti Shield:** Reaction -> Gives a player +2 AC or Fire Resistance against 1 attack.
- **Trickster Spotlight:** Grants Advantage on next attack for 1 party member.
