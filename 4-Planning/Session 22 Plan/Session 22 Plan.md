# The Broken Pact & The Velvet Hat


## 1. Mess Hall Aftermath (15–20 Mins)
* **Goal:** Gear up, establish Mhazhique's loss of magic, and introduce the Hat's compulsion.
* **Key Beats:**
  * Party collects stolen gear from fleeing goblins; Geoff (Bear) recovers.
  * Mhazhique attempts to cast magic -> *Result: Hollow void, spell fails.*
  * Party/Mhazhique attempts to remove the velvet hat -> *Result: Compulsive urge to guard it.*
* **Prompt Question:** *"Mhazhique, as you feel the magic drain away, how does your character physically react to holding onto Dwayne?"*

---

## 2. Moving Dwayne & The Forest Exit (20–30 Mins)
* **Goal:** Choice Node — Getting Dwayne out of the lair and emerging into the forest.
* **Choice Node A (Transporting Dwayne):**
  * [ ] **Brute Force (Gil / Geoff):** Athletics check (DC 12). *Fast, but noisy (alerts Nar'l ahead).*
  * [ ] **Magical Utility (Lord Cock / Flick):** Arcana / Sleight of Hand (DC 12). *Slower, but avoids detection.*

---

## 3. Trilbi’s Pitch & The Contract (20–25 Mins)
* **Goal:** Meet Trilbi in the forest clearing; reveal the hat's true nature.
* **Key Beats:**
  * Trilbi is waiting casually outside.
  * Reveals the hat signed a new patron bargain to replace Ignaroch.
* **Choice Node B (Mhazhique’s Response):**
  * [ ] **Accept Pact:** Unlocks *Trilbi’s Trickster Bolt* (1d10 psychic + 1d4 penalty) immediately for combat.
  * [ ] **Reject / Hesitate:** Mhazhique fights martial/support-only; Trilbi watches playfully from the sidelines.

---

## 4. Boss Ambush: Nar'l & The Ignaroch Reveal (45–60 Mins)
* **Goal:** Tactical combat featuring Nar'l, Shadow Hounds, and Trilbi's interventions.
* **Initiative Order:** 
  * `20` -> **Trilbi's Lair Actions** (Pie Heal, Banana Peel, Confetti Shield, Spotlight)
  * `__` -> Party (Mhazhique, Geoff, Flick, Gil, Lord Cock)
  * `__` -> Nar'l & Shadow Hounds (2)

**Nar'l Combat Reference:**
* **HP:** 38 | **AC:** 15 (*Mage Armor*) | **DC:** 13
* **Key Moves:** *Ignaroch's Retribution* (15ft Cone, 3d6 Fire), *Shatter* (3d8), *Tasha's Mind Whip* (3d6 + Action Restriction), *Fiery Misty Step* (30ft Teleport).
* **Narrative Twist:** Nar'l's spells burn with Ignaroch's signature hellfire — revealing Ignaroch sent him to claim Dwayne's core.

---

## 5. Resolution & Cliffhanger (10–15 Mins)
* **Outcomes:**
  * Nar'l defeated/forced to flee.
  * Mhazhique's status with Trilbi solidified or left as an ongoing negotiation.
* **Ending Hook:** Dwayne's core stabilizes, but a new magical symbol corresponding to Trilbi glows on its surface matching Mhazhique's hat.
