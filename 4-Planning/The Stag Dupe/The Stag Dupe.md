### Ideas
- Involve a giant magical stag
- Flick gets cursed with stag antlers
- Cross planar Tavern crawl
- Ask players to make up stories to tell about Flick on the spot
- Per character challenges
- Party member mind swap (have to play each others characters)
- Memory loss tavern crawl (start of not knowing their character sheets and have to act appropriately. Each tavern offers the opportunity for 1 PC to remember and get their character sheet back)

## The Plan
### Act 1
At some point in the near future maybe, the party finds themselves gathered at [[The Troll's Sack]] for a very special celebration. [[Flick McPlumbs]], one of their own, is getting married! And the party want to celebrate! They have gathered here in their soon-to-open tavern, with a few friends, to play games and share tales, a good hearty send off to wish Flick well in his married life. 

1. The party throws a low-key stag do for Flick, giving him fake antlers to wear, to celebrate his upcoming wedding at [[The Troll's Sack]] with card games, stories and drinking. All while looking forward to the grand opening of their own inn.
2. The guests:
	1. [[Xoblob]]
	2. [[Steve]]
	3. [[Bri'eth Carnor]]
	4. [[Durnan]] (reluctantly)
	5. [[Yagra Stonefist]]
3. Xoblob suggests an old drinking game him and his buddies used to play when they were at the University of Waterdeep:

#### The drinking game
##### Setup
- Each PC gets a small glass filled with **sobriety coins**.
- Empty central pool.
- Each round another player makes up a story about Flick for the table and then they all drink to cheers.
##### Turn Sequence
1. **Cheers!** Player takes a “drink” → make a Con save (DC starts at 10, goes +1 each round to simulate getting tipsier).
2. **Roll the Pour:** Roll 2d6.
    - **On Success:** Choose a player to flick that many coins from their glass into the pool.
    - **On Fail:** Flick that many coins from your own glass into the pool.
##### Passing Out
- When a player’s glass is empty, they “pass out” and are out of the game.
- Last player standing wins the drinking game, but of course, _all_ of them eventually black out in-universe when Flick drops.
##### Extra rules
- **Nat 20**: Player gets the coins their chosen player would have to throw into the central pool (steal)
- **Nat 1**: Empty your glass into the pool bar 1 coin (almost gone)

### Act 2
With the party passed out, the night passes and slowly they all come too.
- Flicks antlers are no longer fake, they are growing out of his head and they're causing him pain and the occasional animalistic outbursts.
- While they're dealing with the real antlers, one player realises "they're not in kansas anymore". Non of their NPC friends are around, (including Dwayne!), and they're not in [[The Troll's Sack]] anymore.
- Exiting the only door they see in the room they're in they find themselves in a circle of 4 trees each with an ornate carving of a door on it. And before them stands a giant mystical stag.
#### The Stag
- The stag's name is Greg Wallace (revealed only if they ask)
- In a booming cockney voice, he asks "who be the horny one", and proclaims he must complete all 4 challenges to earn his betrothed, lest they be lost forever and Flick's antlers remain permanent.
- "Behind these 4 doors you will find a unique challenge each suited to one of your unique skills, fail and the stag will forever be so"

#### The challenges

##### Gil's Challenge - The Keg Raid

###### Scene Setup
- The party steps through the carved door into a vast **brew-hall cavern**, lined with towering ale kegs.
- **Beer Goblins** (foamy little creatures with mugs) rush in squealing: _“Get that ale, lads, quick!”.
- A booming voice bellows: _“Protect the drink, or drown in it!”_

###### Mechanics & Flow
**Goal:** Prevent the goblins from stealing the ale while Gil downs as much as he can.
1. **Beer Goblins (Waves):**
    - Each round, roll **1d4 Beer Goblins** entering the hall.
    - Stats: _AC 12, 1 hp each_ (minions, easy to smash).
    - On death → frothy splash: adjacent PCs must Con save (DC 12) or gain **1 Drunkenness Token**.
2. **Drink the Ale (Gil’s Role):**
    - At the start of each round, Gil may **Chug**: Con save DC 12 + round number.
    - Success → he clears 1 keg (remove token) and must roll on his **Wild Magic Surge table**.
    - Failure → he gains **1 Drunkenness Token**.
3. **Other PCs’ Role:*
    - May fight goblins, plug leaks, or attempt to chug.
    - Their Con saves are harder (DC 14 base) and they risk quick intoxication.

###### Win / Lose Condition
- After **5 rounds**, tally results:
    - **Success:** Gil cleared **3+ kegs** and goblins stole fewer than half.
    - **Failure:** Goblins overwhelm → the brewery floods with ale, sweeping the party out in froth.

###### Fun Flavour Details
- **Gil’s Wild Magic:** Every drink forces a surge (chaos energy + alcohol = double trouble).
- **Beer Goblins:** Slapstick antics — tiny straw-spears, riding rolling barrels, tripping over each other.

##### Geoff's Challenge

###### Scene Setup
- The party enters a **grand menagerie courtyard**: cages, pens, and magical enclosures filled with strange beasts.
- A harried spectral **Zookeeper** runs up, shouting in panic:  
    _“Oi! The beasts are restless! Calm ‘em or they’ll break loose and run wild!”_
- The Stag’s voice booms: _“Order through chaos, or chaos through charm — let’s see what ye pick, little druid.”_    

###### Mechanics & Flow

**Goal:** Geoff must use wit, animal empathy, and trickery to settle the beasts before the menagerie erupts.

1. **Restless Beasts (3 mini-encounters):**
    - Geoff must handle 3 creatures. Each can be dealt with through **animal RP** or **mischievous trickery**.
    - Success = calms the beast. Failure = beast causes slapstick chaos but can be subdued with help.
    
    Example Beasts:
    - **Vain Griffon**: Only calms if given compliments or shiny things.
    - **Joke-Loving Snake**: Demands a knock-knock joke or prank.
    - **Dance-Battle Bear**: Challenges Geoff to a performance contest (Performance vs its +4).
    - **Pickpocket Ferret**: Has stolen the Zookeeper’s keys; Geoff can animal-handle it or match wits in a sleight-of-hand duel.
2. **The Zookeeper’s Keys:**
    - Optional side-mischief: Geoff notices the Zookeeper has a heavy ring of keys… but half are fake.
    - A successful pickpocket check earns the real keys, letting Geoff unlock enclosures safely.
3. **Party Role:**
    - Others can help, but beasts react **worse** to non-druids (higher DC or sillier complications).        
    - Keeps Geoff spotlighted while letting others play along for laughs.

###### Resolution
- **Success:** Geoff charms/tricks 3 beasts → the menagerie calms, animals gather around him adoringly.
- **Partial Success:** 1–2 beasts calm, but others riot. Geoff ends the trial clinging to a stampede, laughing and alive.
- **Failure:** Total chaos. The menagerie collapses in pratfall style (comedic, not lethal), spitting the party out into the hut.    

The Stag declares: _“A knack fer critters, sure… but a knack fer chaos, too. I like it.”_

###### Fun Flavor Details
- **Speak with Animals:** Geoff’s favorite spell shines — the beasts _want_ to talk, but only about ridiculous things.
- **Pickpocketing Flair:** Give him a chance to swipe trinkets from the Zookeeper (which might turn out to be useful “souvenirs”).
- **Comedic Escalation:** Each beast’s demand should get sillier, so the trial builds to a crescendo of chaos.

##### Mhazhique's Challenge - The Bonds that Break us

###### Scene Setup
- The party enters a **volcanic chamber**: cracked basalt floor, lava veins glowing beneath.
- At the center kneels **Dwayne**, unnaturally still, eyes glowing red with possession.
- The Cockney Stag’s voice echoes:  
    _“Even the strongest of bonds can turn to chains, lad. Break ‘em… or be broken.”_
- Without warning, Dwayne rises and attacks

###### Mechanics & Flow
**Goal:** Defeat or overcome the possessed Dwayne.

1. **Temporary Relief:**
    - At the start of the challenge, Mhazhique feels a wave of calm.
    - For this trial, he **does not roll Stress checks** (narratively, the stag has “freed” him).
    - This relief feels good, almost addictive — a dangerous temptation.
2. **The Fight:**
    - Dwayne fights as an **Earth Elemental** but reflavored with fiery cracks and glowing eyes.
    - His abilities are corrupted: slam attacks explode with sparks, tremors shake lava vents.
    - PCs can all help, but Dwayne focuses his aggression on Mhazhique.
3. **Choices in Combat:**
    - **Strike him down:** Straightforward fight to “kill” Dwayne.
    - **Try to quell him:**
        - Skill challenge (3 successes before 2 fails).
        - Persuasion, Insight, Arcana, even Fire magic repurposed as cleansing flames.
        - Fail = forced back into combat.

###### Resolution
- **If Dwayne is “killed”:**
    - His body crumbles into rubble… then re-forms, restored and freed.
    - The stag praises Mhazhique for his resolve: _“Sometimes ye must break what ye love, to save it.”_
- **If Dwayne is “quelled”:**
    - The red glow fades, possession cracks.
    - Dwayne collapses, then rises again, unharmed.
    - The stag commends: _“Mercy takes more strength than fury. Ye chose well.”_

Either way → reward is the same: **Dwayne returns purified, stronger, and his bond with Mhazhique deepens**.

###### Fun Flavor Details
- **Stress Relief:** Play up how _good_ it feels for Mhazhique not to be burdened by stress checks in this fight. At the end, the stag says something sly like:  
    _“Relief feels grand, doesn’t it? But life’s trials don’t give ye that luxury.”_  
    — a reminder that growth comes from struggle.
- **Visuals:** Every hit against Dwayne cracks his rocky hide → glowing magma bleeds out like blood.
- **Emotional Twist:** Don’t reveal Dwayne’s revival until the end. Let the fight feel like a real, permanent sacrifice.

###### Dwayne's Stats for Mhazhique's challenge
_Large elemental, chaotic neutral (possessed)_

**Armor Class:** 17 (natural armor)  
**Hit Points:** ~120 (keep flexible, since this is a story-driven fight)  
**Speed:** 30 ft., burrow 30 ft.

**STR** 20 (+5) | **DEX** 8 (-1) | **CON** 20 (+5) | **INT** 5 (-3) | **WIS** 10 (+0) | **CHA** 6 (-2)

**Saving Throws:** Str +8, Con +8  
**Damage Resistances:** Bludgeoning, Piercing, Slashing (nonmagical)  
**Damage Immunities:** Poison, Fire  
**Condition Immunities:** Exhaustion, Paralyzed, Poisoned  
**Senses:** Darkvision 60 ft., Tremorsense 60 ft., Passive Perception 10  
**Languages:** Terran (possessed growls)

**Traits**
- **Possessed Fury.** At the start of each turn, Dwayne targets Mhazhique if possible.
- **Volcanic Body.** When Dwayne takes damage, sparks burst: creatures within 5 ft. must succeed on a DC 13 Dex save or take 1d6 fire damage.
- **Trial Bound.** If reduced to 0 HP or calmed by skill challenge, Dwayne collapses → re-forms purified.    

**Actions**
- **Multiattack.** Two Slam attacks.
- **Slam.** +8 to hit, reach 10 ft., 14 (2d8+5) bludgeoning + 1d6 fire.
- **Lava Tremor (Recharge 5–6).** Dwayne slams the ground; each creature within 20 ft. must make a DC 15 Dex save or take 14 (4d6) fire damage and fall prone. The ground cracks, glowing with magma.    

**Legendary-ish Twist (Optional)**
If you want to add drama, give him **one special phase-change** at half HP:
- He cracks apart, spewing lava. All creatures make a DC 15 Con save or gain the _frightened_ condition until the end of their next turn.

##### Flick's Challenge

###### Scene Setup
- The carved door opens into a **grand, ghostly banquet hall** lit by torches of green flame.
- The tables are filled with **spectral half-orc nobles and warriors**, dressed for a wedding feast.
- The Cockney Stag bellows:  
    _“Love ain’t just fightin’ and bleedin’, lad — it’s speakin’ plain and true. Woo this lot, or lose yer betrothed forever!”_    
- The half-orcs raise goblets and demand a **wedding toast** from Flick.

###### Mechanics & Flow

**Goal:** Flick must win over the ghostly audience with charm, heart, or ridiculous antics.

1. **The Toast (Skill Challenge):**
    - Flick delivers a speech — 3 successes before 2 failures.
    - Checks: Persuasion, Performance, History (stories of past deeds), even Acrobatics if he turns it into physical comedy.
    - On a **failure**, he fumbles (wrong name, spills drink, blurts something awkward). The audience jeers — but every fumble can still be spun into comedy or charm.
2. **Audience Demands:**
    - Between rolls, ghostly half-orcs heckle with challenges:
        - _“Tell us yer finest victory!”_
        - _“Prove yer strength!”_
        - _“Make us laugh, boy!”_
    - Flick must improvise, leaning into awkward over-confidence.
3. **Party Role:**
    - Allies can chip in (e.g., prompting stories, playing music, doing pratfalls), but each assist risks upping the DC (“now ye’ve dragged yer mates into it, impress us all the more!”).
    - Keeps spotlight on Flick but lets others join the fun.        

###### Resolution
- **Success:** The hall erupts in cheers; the half-orc ghosts lift Flick up, chanting his name. The Stag declares:  
    _“Clumsy words, but a true heart. That’s all that matters.”_
- **Partial Success (funny failure):** The ghosts laugh at Flick’s disastrous charm but accept his sincerity anyway. They toast him in good humor.
- **Failure:** The ghosts boo — until one pipes up: _“Ah, he’s bloody hopeless, but he’s one of us!”_ The crowd roars with laughter, still granting success (because sincerity > polish).    

###### Fun Flavour Details

- **Awkward Toast Table (d6 Fumbles):**
    1. Calls someone by the wrong name.
    2. Knocks over the wedding cake.
    3. Tells an embarrassing story about himself.
    4. Mixes up the bride’s family with goblins.
    5. Starts crying mid-toast.
    6. Overconfident boast that falls flat.
- **The Ghostly Half-Orcs:** Make them rowdy but warm-hearted — they _want_ Flick to succeed, but in a trial-by-embarrassment way.    
- **Symbolic End:** His spectral antlers glow, then dissolve into light, leaving a faint mark on his forehead.

### Act 3 - Challenge complete
With all 4 challenges completed, the party returns to the forest, to the cockney stag and proclaims they have completed the challenges.
- The stag congratulates them, leans in towards flick and touches his antlers with their own, and the antlers begin to dissipate magically. 
- The stag consoles Mhazhique on the loss of Dwayne if he killed him, and surprises him with a fully healthy Dwayne who emerges from behind the stag. 
- They door to the cabin they came out of opens, and through it they see [[The Troll's Sack]]. Bustling and bright, it seems while they've been gone, the opening night has come around. The party hears [[Bri'eth Carnor]] proclaim **"Ladies and Gentleman, for your entertainment on this opening night, please welcome, THE MIGHNUTS"** and the session ends.
