### Ideas
- Involve a giant magical stag
- Flick gets cursed with stag antlers
- Cross planar Tavern crawl
- Ask players to make up stories to tell about Flick on the spot
- Per character challenges
- Party member mind swap (have to play each others characters)
- Memory loss tavern crawl (start of not knowing their character sheets and have to act appropriately. Each tavern offers the opportunity for 1 PC to remember and get their character sheet back)

## The Plan
### Act 1
1. The party throws a low-key stag do for Flick, giving him fake antlers to wear, to celebrate his upcoming wedding at [[The Troll's Sack]] with card games, stories and drinking. All while looking forward to the grand opening of their own inn.
2. Tankard in the middle of the table, every time a PC takes a drink, the player flicks a token (d&d coin) into the tankard. If they fail the drinking DC, they flick another. When it's full the party blacks out.
