You all awaken, with sore heads, from the strangest dream about a stag party for Flick. Chained in a dark cell, stripped of all but your clothes (**perception check DC 5** from anyone to notice [[Mhazhique Waand|Mhazhique]] is wearing the hat ([[Trilbi's Hat]]) from the dream), you hear **voices of goblins** down a distant corridor from the same direction as the only beam of light you see, peaking through a small iron barred window in a thick wooden door. The sound of dripping water fills gaps of silence as you all start to try and get your bearings.

## Act 1
Wisdom roll **DC 10** to avoid exhaustion from "bad dream".
### Goblin Chatter - Perception Check
- **DC 5** - Talk of keeping them until [[Nar'l Xibrindas]] returns, he'll know what to do with them.
- **DC 10** - Mentions of another cell housing [[Dwayne the Elemental|Dwayne]], down the corridor.
- **DC 15** - Arguments over who gets the fancy hat ([[Trilbi's Hat]]) when the prisoners are dealt with - and the struggle they had to try get it off [[Mhazhique Waand|Mhazhique]] in the first place.
### Escape
- If [[Mhazhique Waand|Mhazhique]] tries to tap into his connection with Dwayne (**Wisdom DC 15**) he can perform an action similar to the message cantrip, willing Dwayne to do something, maybe try break out or cause a distraction.
- If [[Gil Lioth|Gil]] rages, his wild magic surge could potentially help.
- [[Flick McPlumbs|Flick]] could use his Echo Knight ability, but being an echo of himself, also **wouldn't have his gear**.
- On a perception check, [[Drewid 'Geoff' Barrymore|Geoff]] could listen out for rats or similar creatures to use Speak with Animals to try get help.
- [[Lord Maji Cock|Lord Cock]] could use any of his spells he has prepared that only require verbal or somatic components.
- Any of the party is free to make a strength check **DC 18** to try break free of their chains.
- The goblin the group hired as a mole will try free them from their cell when the coast is clear, if they haven't already escaped.
### Things of Note
- [[Mhazhique Waand|Mhazhique]] would notice he still has a feeling of emptiness and lack of power from the events of the "dream".
- Perhaps the dream and it's events was a consequence of [[Mhazhique Waand|Mhazhique]]'s row with his patron.
- [[Mhazhique Waand|Mhazhique]] has to attune to the hat he is wearing before he is bestowed powers by [[Trilbi the Magnificent]], his surprise new patron, who is an arch enemy of his seemingly former patron.
- He is met by [[Trilbi the Magnificent]] once more, who explains the situation, and offers him a choice. He can restore his former power, mimicking that of his former patron. Or he can "re-spec" to something more Trilbi-esque in the realm of mischief and trickery. ([[Mhazhique Re-spec]])
