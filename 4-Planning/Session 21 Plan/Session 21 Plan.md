You all awaken, with sore heads, from the strangest dream about a stag party for Flick. Chained in a dark cell on a damp stone floor, stripped of all but your clothes.

You can hear, furniture scraping, laughter and voices of goblins down a distant corridor from the same direction as the only source of light you see, a flickering torch peaking through a small iron barred window in a thick wooden door set into the stone wall. The sound of dripping water fills gaps of silence as you all start to try and get your bearings. The air is thick with the smell of Goblins, sweat, old-blood and badly cooked meat. A rat darts across the floor disappearing into a crack in the stone in the corner of the room.

### To begin
- [[Mhazhique Waand|Mhazhique]] would notice he still has a feeling of emptiness and lack of power from the events of the "dream".
- **Perception check DC 10** from anyone to notice a note scrawled on paper left by [[Goblin Dave]]
	- "Waiting for right guard change to let you out, too busy this morning, I best get paid well for this, - Dayv"
- **Perception check DC 5** from anyone to notice [[Mhazhique Waand|Mhazhique]] is wearing the hat ([[Trilbi's Hat]]) from the dream
- **Wisdom roll DC 10** to avoid exhaustion from "bad dream" - **beating an 18 will reveal recognition of the effects of a modified sleep spell and a memory of entering a room filled with smoke (sleep cloud)**.

### Dwayne
Dwayne is being kept in a cell just off the mess hall, the party can't get to him without going through the mess hall first.
- If [[Drewid 'Geoff' Barrymore|Geoff]] becomes a rat and follows the crack in the cell, they will come out in Dwayne's cell.
He is in a state of muddy depression, only communication with [[Mhazhique Waand]] can rile him up and kick him into gear to fight.
### Gear retrieval 
- The party's weapons are being used by goblins in the mess hall in a make-shift fighting pit. Some goblins are betting on results.
- Their armour and other effects are being kept in a storage room just off the mess hall.
### Fight in the mess hall
If the party break out whilst there are still goblins in the guild hall (didn't wait for ([[Goblin Dave]])) there will be an encounter to be had. **3d4 goblins present**.
- DM Roll **1d6** - on a 5 or 6, Dwayne is being kept in a cage in the middle of the mess hall and the goblins are taunting/torturing him with the parties weapons and some of their own.
The goblins aren't organised and tactical, roll **1d6** alongside initiative  for each goblin on their first turn to determine their course of action:
- **1** - Panic
- **2** - Argue
- **3** - Flee
- **4** - Attacking
- **5** - Defensive
- **6** - Scream about "the hatted one"
### Leaving the HQ
As soon as the party leave the vicinity of the HQ, [[Trilbi the Magnificent]] is immediately aware of the hat's location and **BAMPF** appears in front of the party when they start to settling and get their bearings.

He will explain what's going on to the party somewhat and offer Mhazhique a deal. He can restore his former power, mimicking that of his former patron. Or he can "re-spec" to something more Trilbi-esque in the realm of mischief and trickery. See [[Mhazhique Re-spec]].

Ingaroch makes a vocal only appearance, threatening the whole party, and swearing revenge for Mhazhique's betrayal of his "gifts".
