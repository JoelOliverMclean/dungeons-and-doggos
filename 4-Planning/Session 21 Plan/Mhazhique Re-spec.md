#### **Option 1: Keep Old Flame**

_Trilbi restores the same subclass flavor but through his lens._

- **Subclass:** Fiend (Warlock)
    
- **Visuals:** Fire becomes theatrical—multicolored sparks, illusory smoke faces, mocking laughter in every flame.
    
- **Tone:** Same mechanics, new presentation. Power feels like borrowed flair, not burning wrath.
    

#### **Option 2: The Mischief Re-spec**

_Embrace Trilbi’s domain of trickery and illusion._

- **Subclass:** Archfey
