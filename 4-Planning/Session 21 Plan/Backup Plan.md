In a previous ad-hoc session due to player absence, I improvised and started to take the player's on the start of an adventure get [[Gil Lioth|Gil]] his [[Tankard of Plenty]]. They heard rumour from [[Embric]] at the [[The Steam and Steel]] of some disgruntled workers of a notorious baron in a pub that the baron owned the [[Tankard of Plenty]]. The party then went to this pub to see if these workers were there and get information from them about it. They ended up singing karaoke and eventually some of the workers came in. Gil challenged them to some axe throwing and as a result, one of the workers agreed to meet him at the baron's manor late the next night and he'd help him in so he could steal the tankard.

# The Tankard of Plenty Heist

## Session Premise

The party arrives expecting a quiet late-night burglary with help from a disgruntled worker.

Instead:  
Baron Aldwyn Vexley is hosting an extravagant soirée at the manor.

Music.  
Guests.  
Guards.  
Servants.  
A magical ale fountain centerpiece powered by the Tankard of Plenty.

The worker absolutely did not know this was happening.

---

# Tone

Fantasy heist comedy with escalating magical disaster.

The players should feel:

- underprepared,
    
- improvisational,
    
- constantly almost caught,
    
- and eventually trapped inside a collapsing noble disaster.
    

---

# The Tankard Setup

The Tankard of Plenty is the centerpiece of a massive enchanted ale fountain.

Mounted at the top of an elaborate brass mechanism.

The Baron has magically overclocked it to continuously refill beyond its intended limits.

It is becoming unstable.

Visual signs:

- glowing runes,
    
- pressure hissing,
    
- magical foam,
    
- ale spurting unpredictably,
    
- vibrating metal,
    
- servants increasingly nervous.
    

---

# Escalation

## Stage 1 — Weirdness

- mugs overflowing,
    
- ale changing colour,
    
- random drunken magical effects,
    
- burps producing sparks,
    
- guests loving it.
    

## Stage 2 — Magical Stress

- runes cracking,
    
- pressure building,
    
- humming audible,
    
- magical intoxication intensifies,
    
- servants start panicking.
    

## Stage 3 — Disaster Imminent

- ale geysers,
    
- fountain shaking violently,
    
- magical flooding,
    
- ale creatures forming,
    
- arcane discharge.
    

The party must decide:

- steal the Tankard,
    
- stabilize it,
    
- save guests,
    
- or escape chaos.
